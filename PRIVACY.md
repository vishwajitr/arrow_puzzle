# Arrow Escape – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 Nov 2026

The Arrow Escape game itself does not collect personal information. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to deliver them, as described below.

## About this policy

This privacy policy applies to the Arrow Escape mobile game for Android (the "App"), developed and published by Vishwajit ("I", "me", or "the Developer"). It explains what information the App handles and how. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

## Information the App collects

The App does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and it does not require an account or sign-in.

The game data it keeps is stored only on your device:

* Progress: which levels you have cleared and your stars
* Hint tokens
* Settings: sound, haptics, theme and style

The Developer cannot see or access this data. If Android backup is turned on for your device, it may be included in your personal device backup, which is managed by Google under your account.

## Advertising

The App shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner below the puzzle, a full-screen ad between some levels, an optional video you can choose to watch for a hint, and occasionally a full-screen ad when you return to the App. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

* Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID
* Approximate location, derived from your IP address
* Device and app information, such as device model, OS version and app version
* Ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic data

In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it is required, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's use of this data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Permissions and third parties

The App uses internet access, network state and the advertising ID permission only to load ads. It requests no other sensitive permissions. Vibration is used only for haptic feedback during play.

Apart from Google AdMob, the App shares no data with any third party, and it does not sell data.

## Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
